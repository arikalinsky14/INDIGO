Upload a material (CSV)

      
    

    
A refractive-index spectrum (n, k against wavelength). The server
      interpolates it onto the 128-point frequency grid used in training and inference.

    

      

      

      

    

    

      expected format
      
wavelength_nm,n,k
300,1.479,0.0
350,1.476,0.0
400,1.473,0.0
…
900,1.464,0.0

      
    
        
  • Header row required. The first column may be wavelength_nm or nm.
    • 
        
  • k may be omitted or zero for non-absorbing dielectrics.
    • 
        
  • Wavelengths outside the file's range take the nearest endpoint's value.
    • 
        
  • The server returns n, k on the 128-point grid (uniform in frequency, 300-900 nm).
    • 
        
  • The pool holds at most 32 materials; deselect one if needed.

How a solve works

      
    

    
solve(model, pool, spec) → Result,
      in the order below. Settings you can change are in blue.


    
    
      
  1. 1
    
        
    Parse (Prompt tab only)
    
        
    The text goes through the LLM parser (parse.py), which
        returns a target Lab and a constraint list; schema, semantic and physical checks reject
        malformed output.
    2. 
      
  2. 2
    
        
    Encode the pool
    
        
    Up to M_MAX = 32 materials are encoded into the model's
        slot representation, once per request.
    3. 
      
  3. 3
    
        
    Sample: Ensemble N, Temperature
    
        
    The model decodes N candidate stacks for the target and
        pool. Constraint masks apply while decoding, so every candidate is feasible.
    4. 
      
  4. 4
    
        
    Simulate
    
        
    Each distinct candidate goes through the transfer-matrix simulator for its reflectance,
        Lab and ΔE₀₀ to the target.
    5. 
      
  5. 5
    
        
    Select: Top-k, Tolerance %, Weight λ
    
        
    The best Top-k are kept. With tolerance 0 (the paper)
        they are ranked by ΔE₀₀ alone; above 0 by J = ΔE + λ·R, a robustness-weighted objective.
    6. 
      
  6. 6
    
        
    Refine: Refine top-N, Refine steps
    
        
    Each refined candidate's thicknesses are tuned by gradient steps (DoG) to lower ΔE₀₀,
        within the constraints. Top-N 0 refines all of the top-k, as the paper does.
    7. 
      
  7. 7
    
        
    Finalise: MC samples
    
        
    With MC samples above 0, random thickness perturbations measure robustness. The best
        candidate is returned with its provenance (checkpoint hash, seed, settings).
    8. 
    


    

      
Presets

      
    
        
  • Paper: N 250, top-10, all 10 refined × 100 steps, ΔE₀₀ alone. The setting behind every number in the paper.
    • 
        
  • Fast and Balanced: smaller searches; expect a higher ΔE₀₀.
    • 
        
  • Robust: a different objective (ΔE + λ·R at 5% tolerance, 16 MC draws); trades colour accuracy for thickness tolerance.
