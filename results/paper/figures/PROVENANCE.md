One line per figure: script, inputs, fields.

- **Fig 1** `scripts/figures/fig1_pipeline.py` -> `fig1_pipeline.{pdf,png}` (17 cm) and `toc_graphic.{tiff,pdf}` (8 x 4 cm, 600 dpi). Schematic, no data. Reals: JaxLayerLumos 0.3.3 CSVs via load_jll_directory, split by create_dataset/src/pool_sampler.py split_jll_real (26 held-in, 8 held-out = HELD_OUT_REAL_MATERIALS). Cloud: 6000 draws of pool_sampler._sample_synthetic with PoolSamplerConfig.synthetic_weights (0.25, 0.25, 0.15, 0.35) on the held-in reals, seed 0 (lorentz 2158, perturb 2946, interp 896). Al2O3* plotted as loaded (n = 0.84, k = 5.70): the upstream Al2O3-Querry-1985.csv holds metal-like values in the visible. (b, c) `results/prod_eval/eval_tier_{a,b}_greedy.json` results[].{gt_lab, structure_source} only; tiers are SEPARATE PANELS (b = tier A, c = tier B), color = source. Tier A n = 1968 plotted of 2000 (random 1445, high-chroma 523); tier B n = 1987 of 2000 (random 1484, high-chroma 503). Dropped as non-physical Lab (L* outside [0,100] or C* > 150): 32 (A), 13 (B). High-chroma share of all targets: 26.8% (A), 25.5% (B). Rings at C* = 8 and 30.
